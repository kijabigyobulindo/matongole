Our story

Born from community. Built for change.

Shalom Namutere Project was established in Namutere, Samia Bugwe North County, Busia District, to bring peace, harmony and meaningful transformation to eight underserved villages.

At the heart of our work is a simple idea: give young people relevant skills and room to grow, and they can become a powerful force for progress in their own communities.
