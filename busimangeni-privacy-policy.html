1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Busimangeni (also known as The Number Rush), an Android mathematics puzzle game developed and published by KIJABIGYO, the innovation and ICT department of Shalom Namutere Project, Uganda.

We designed Busimangeni as an offline game that can be played without providing personal information.

2. Information we collect

Busimangeni does not collect, transmit, or request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photographs, or device identifiers.

The game does not include user accounts, analytics tracking, advertising, or in-app purchases. We do not monitor individual players or build player profiles.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide basic gameplay features, Busimangeni may save your high scores, game progress, and preferences locally in the app's private storage on your device. This information is used only to support gameplay and display your progress.

KIJABIGYO does not receive this locally stored information through the app. It is not uploaded to a KIJABIGYO server or shared with other players.

4. Data sharing and third-party services

Busimangeni does not share player information with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties. The game does not integrate advertising or analytics services.

Downloading or updating the app through Google Play may involve separate processing of information by Google under Google's own privacy terms. Such processing is not controlled by Busimangeni.

5. Device permissions and internet access

Busimangeni is designed to function without an internet connection and does not request Android runtime permissions to access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, or other sensitive device features.

The app does not need an account or network access for its core gameplay.

6. Data security

Because the game does not collect or transmit personal information, KIJABIGYO does not maintain a remote database of player information. Game-related preferences and scores are stored using the app's local storage facilities, subject to the security protections of your device and Android operating system.

No electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We encourage players to keep their devices and operating systems updated.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local game scores and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game, subject to your device's operating-system backup and restore settings.

You can generally remove the game's local data through Android Settings → Apps → Busimangeni → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage may reset your progress and personal best scores.

Since the game does not create accounts or maintain player records on KIJABIGYO servers, there is no online player account or server-side game profile to delete.

8. Age suitability

Busimangeni is intended for individuals aged 13 and above. It features mathematics puzzles and does not require users to submit personal information. It does not offer social networking, public chat, or user-generated content features.

The age guidance stated here describes the developer's intended audience and is not an official Google Play content rating.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or privacy practices change, or if applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when we publish an updated policy. If we introduce data collection or third-party services in a future version, we will update our disclosures and any required Google Play declarations before release.
